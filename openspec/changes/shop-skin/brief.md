## Wish

"Dass man Villagern auch noch, wenn man möchte, einen Player-Skin geben kann, also so einen richtigen, wie auch in der Lobby, dass man da eine ID oder sowas eingibt und dann ein Skin gesetzt wird."

## Decisions

| # | Question | Answer | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | How is a skin possible at all? | A villager cannot wear one, so a shop with a skin stands as a mannequin, like the lobby npcs | user |
| Q2 | Who sets it, and how? | The owning team in the shop editor, by Minecraft player name, like `/npc skin` | user |
| Q3 | What about shops without a skin? | They stay villagers and do not change | user |
| Q4 | How is the name entered, and how is a skin removed? | A button in the shop editor asks in chat; `aus` removes the skin, `abbrechen` keeps it | delegated |
| Q5 | What is a valid name? | The format `/npc skin` accepts; an unknown account shows the default look, as in the lobby | delegated |

## Out of scope

- Skins from a texture URL.
- Admin-only skins or a skin filter.
